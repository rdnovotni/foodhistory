# Initial article starter briefs

These are deliberately unpublishable research briefs, not finished articles. Import or copy one into the private editor only when a researcher is ready to replace every bracketed instruction with sourced prose, canonical entity links, and canonical citations.

Suggested order:

1. `apple-pie.md` exercises a normalized food entity and menu occurrences.
2. `restaurant-menus-as-evidence.md` establishes the project's evidence-reading method.
3. `soda-bottles-as-food-history.md` connects material culture, marks, manufacturing, holdings, and provenance.

Do not turn placeholders into generalized claims from memory. Preserve uncertainty, geographic scope, historical wording, and disagreements between sources.
